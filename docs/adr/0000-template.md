# ADR NNNN: Short decision title

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Owner:** Repository maintainer or appointed decision owner
- **Related ticket:** `KHEPER-xxx`

## Context

Describe the problem, constraints, affected users or systems, and why a durable
decision is required. Identify offline/privacy, accessibility, data migration,
localization/direction, licensing, scholarly-content, and release implications
where applicable.

## Decision

State the chosen option precisely, including boundaries and ownership.

## Rationale

Explain why this option best satisfies the accepted constraints and evidence.

## Alternatives considered

### Alternative A

Describe the option and why it was not chosen.

### Alternative B

Describe the option and why it was not chosen.

## Consequences

Record positive and negative consequences, operational burden, security/privacy
effects, accessibility effects, licences, and follow-up work.

## Migration and rollback

Describe adoption steps, compatibility, data/content migration, rollback, and
the trigger for reconsidering the decision. Use `Not applicable — <reason>`
when justified.

## Evidence and approvals

Link tests, prototypes, benchmarks, source/review records, and the approving
roles. A committed draft is not specialist approval.
